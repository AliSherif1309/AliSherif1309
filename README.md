## About me

I'm a biomedical engineer based in Doha, Qatar, working in field service for medical diagnostic systems. My work brings together equipment maintenance, troubleshooting, and software that makes service tasks easier.

- **Diagnostic systems:** Liaison XL, Erytra Eflexis, and Procleix Panther.
- **Software:** tools for finding technical documents, supporting service workflows, and automating repetitive work.
- **Community:** practical learning resources for biomedical equipment engineers.
